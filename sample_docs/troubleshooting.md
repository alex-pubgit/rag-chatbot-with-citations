# Troubleshooting swaps

Sample data: AcmeSwap is a fictional product, used to demonstrate this chatbot.

## A swap is stuck as pending

A pending swap is waiting to be included in a block. Look up the transaction hash in the network's block explorer to see its status. While it is still pending you can speed it up or cancel it from your wallet.

## A swap failed

Most failed swaps are caused by slippage: the price moved by more than your tolerance. A swap also fails when your wallet does not hold enough of the network's native coin to pay the network fee.

## Tokens do not show in the wallet

Wallets do not always list new tokens automatically. Add the token to your wallet by its contract address to make the balance visible.
