# Security

Sample data: AcmeSwap is a fictional product, used to demonstrate this chatbot.

## Your keys are yours

AcmeSwap is non-custodial, so only you control your wallet. AcmeSwap cannot recover a lost seed phrase. Write the seed phrase down on paper and keep it offline.

## Transactions are final

AcmeSwap cannot reverse or cancel a confirmed transaction. Check the token, the amount and the network before you confirm.

## Approvals

A swap asks your wallet to approve the token you are selling. Approve only the amount you intend to swap, and revoke approvals you no longer need.
