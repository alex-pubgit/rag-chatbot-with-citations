# Contacting support

Sample data: AcmeSwap is a fictional product, used to demonstrate this chatbot.

## Official channels

Official support is available in two places only: the help widget inside the app and the public #support channel. Support is free.

## How to spot fake support

Team members never send the first direct message. They never ask for your seed phrase or your private key. There is no wallet validation, sync or rectification tool, and any site that offers one is a scam.

## What to include in a request

Describe what happened and include the network and the transaction hash. A transaction hash is public and safe to share. Your seed phrase and private key are not.
