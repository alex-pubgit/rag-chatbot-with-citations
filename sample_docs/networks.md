# Supported networks

Sample data: AcmeSwap is a fictional product, used to demonstrate this chatbot.

## Networks

AcmeSwap works on three networks: BNB Smart Chain, Ethereum and Polygon. You can switch between them in the network menu at the top of the app.

## Native coins

Each network has its own native coin, which you need for network fees: BNB on BNB Smart Chain, ETH on Ethereum and POL on Polygon. Keep a small balance of the native coin in your wallet, or your transactions cannot be sent.

## Tokens sent on the wrong network

AcmeSwap cannot recover tokens that were sent on a network it does not support.
