# Fees

Sample data: AcmeSwap is a fictional product, used to demonstrate this chatbot.

## Swap fee

The swap fee is 0.25% of the amount you swap, on every supported network. Swapping one stablecoin for another stablecoin costs less: the fee for stablecoin-to-stablecoin swaps is 0.10%.

## Network fees

Network fees, also called gas, are separate from the swap fee. Gas is paid in the native coin of the network you are using, and it goes to the network's validators, not to AcmeSwap.

## Where to see the fees

Every quote lists the swap fee and the estimated network fee before you confirm.
