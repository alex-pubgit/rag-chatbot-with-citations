# Staking

Sample data: AcmeSwap is a fictional product, used to demonstrate this chatbot.

## How staking works

You can stake ACME tokens to earn rewards. Staked tokens stay in the staking contract until you unstake them.

## Rewards

Staking rewards are distributed every Monday at 12:00 UTC. Reward rates vary from week to week and are not guaranteed.

## Unstaking

Unstaking takes 7 days. Tokens in the unstaking period do not earn rewards, and they cannot be transferred until the 7 days have passed.
