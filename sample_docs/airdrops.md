# Airdrops

Sample data: AcmeSwap is a fictional product, used to demonstrate this chatbot.

## Current status

No airdrop is currently announced. If that changes, it will be announced on the official channels first.

## Airdrop scams

Any airdrop claim page is a scam unless the airdrop was announced on the official channels. Scam pages ask you to connect your wallet and sign a transaction that drains it. AcmeSwap will never ask you to pay a fee to receive an airdrop.
