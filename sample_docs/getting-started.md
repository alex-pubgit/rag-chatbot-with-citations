# Getting started

Sample data: AcmeSwap is a fictional product, used to demonstrate this chatbot.

## What AcmeSwap is

AcmeSwap is a non-custodial app for swapping tokens. Non-custodial means your tokens stay in your own wallet until the moment of the swap, and AcmeSwap never holds your keys.

## Your first swap

Connect your wallet, pick the token you want to sell and the token you want to buy, and review the quote. The quote shows the swap fee, the estimated network fee and the minimum amount you will receive. Confirm the swap in your wallet to send it.

## Limits

The smallest amount you can swap is 1 USDT equivalent. There is no maximum, but large swaps move the price more, so check the quote before you confirm.
